Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Short math and safety answers. Math only — no dose recommendations.

How do I convert mg to mcg?

1 mg = 1,000 mcg. To convert milligrams (mg) to micrograms (mcg), multiply by 1,000 (for example, 0.25 mg × 1,000 = 250 mcg). To convert micrograms (mcg) to milligrams (mg), divide by 1,000 (for example, 500 mcg ÷ 1,000 = 0.5 mg).

What if my dose does not fit in my syringe?

Use a larger syringe (such as 0.5 mL or 1.0 mL) or add more BAC water — the calculator warns you in red whenever your dose exceeds your selected syringe capacity so you never draw beyond the maximum fill line.

How long does a mixed vial last?

Mixed peptides are generally used within 28–30 days under refrigeration. The calculator flags a vial that would last longer with a yellow safety warning so you can consider reconstituting smaller quantities.

Why does the calculator show no result yet?

It waits until all inputs are filled (vial size, BAC water volume, and desired dose). This safety guard ensures you never see a partial, misleading, or incorrect draw calculation.
