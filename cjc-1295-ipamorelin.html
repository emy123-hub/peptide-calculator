Protocol & Expiry Tracking

Monitoring blend vial longevity, dosing schedule, and the 28–30 day discard rule.

Once bacteriostatic water (containing 0.9% benzyl alcohol) is introduced to the vial, laboratory standards generally establish an in-use shelf life of 28 to 30 days when stored continuously under refrigeration at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C).

Mathematics

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Blend Mathematics

Formulas and dilution mechanics for combination blend vials.

A CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin blend vial contains two distinct peptides combined into a single lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder mixture within a sterile glass vial. When calculating reconstitution parameters, the total quantity of powder in milligrams is the governing value.

For example, a common 5 mg + 5 mg blend vial contains 5 mg of CJC-1295 and 5 mg of Ipamorelin, providing a total of 10 mg of active powder. In the calculator, enter 10 mg as the vial size because both peptides dissolve simultaneously into the exact same volume of bacteriostatic water.

The calculation sequence follows three core mathematical formulas:

  1. Total Solution Concentration (mg/mL): Total powder mass (mg) divided by the added bacteriostatic water volume (mL). For example: 10 mg ÷ 2 mL = 5 mg/mL (5,000 mcg/mL total blend concentration).
  2. Draw Volume (mL): Desired dose in milligrams divided by the solution concentration. For a 500 mcg (0.5 mg) dose: 0.5 mg ÷ 5 mg/mL = 0.10 mL.
  3. Syringe Calibration (Units): Liquid volume multiplied by the syringe units per milliliter. On a standard U-100 syringe (100 units/mL): 0.10 mL × 100 = 10 units.

This process is strictly mathematical. No medical, therapeutic, or dosage recommendations are provided; always follow licensed clinical instructions.

Common CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin Blend Vial Sizes

Comparing 5 mg and 10 mg blend vials and liquid dilution concentrations.

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin blends are commonly supplied in total powder amounts of 5 mg (such as 2.5 mg + 2.5 mg) and 10 mg (such as 5 mg + 5 mg). Because the lyophilized powder dissolves without adding measurable liquid volume, the added BAC water volume directly dictates the concentration:

  • 5 mg Total Blend Vial:
    • Reconstituted with 1 mL BAC water: Concentration is 5 mg/mL (5,000 mcg/mL), yielding 50 mcg per unit on a U-100 syringe.
    • Reconstituted with 2 mL BAC water: Concentration is 2.5 mg/mL (2,500 mcg/mL), yielding 25 mcg per unit on a U-100 syringe.
  • 10 mg Total Blend Vial:
    • Reconstituted with 2 mL BAC water: Concentration is 5 mg/mL (5,000 mcg/mL), yielding 50 mcg per unit on a U-100 syringe.
    • Reconstituted with 3 mL BAC water: Concentration is 3.33 mg/mL (3,333 mcg/mL), yielding 33.3 mcg per unit on a U-100 syringe.

Dilution mechanics: Adding more BAC water dilutes the solution, giving more syringe units for easier visual alignment of fine measurements. Adding less BAC water creates a higher concentration requiring fewer units to draw.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin blend reconstitution math, measurements, and stability facts.

How many units is 500 mcg from a 10 mg blend vial?

In a 10 mg blend vial reconstituted with 2 mL BAC water, the concentration is 5 mg/mL, so 500 mcg (0.5 mg) = 0.10 mL = 10 units on a standard U-100 syringe (0.10 mL × 100 units/mL = 10 units).

The exact unit count changes depending on how the vial is mixed. For example, if the same 10 mg vial is mixed with 3 mL BAC water, the concentration is 3.33 mg/mL, and a 500 mcg dose requires 0.15 mL (15 units). Use the calculator above to verify your specific reconstitution volume.

What does a 5 mg + 5 mg blend vial mean?

A "5 mg + 5 mg" blend vial contains 5 mg of CJC-1295 and 5 mg of Ipamorelin combined in the same vial, which equals 10 mg of total powder. Because both compounds are dissolved together in the liquid, the calculator uses the total powder weight (10 mg) to determine the concentration and draw volume.

Is 500 mcg the same as 0.5 mg?

Yes: 1000 mcg = 1 mg (pure metric conversion). Therefore, 500 mcg ÷ 1,000 = 0.5 mg. To convert milligrams to micrograms, multiply by 1,000 (0.5 mg × 1,000 = 500 mcg).

How long can a mixed vial be used?

Once reconstituted with bacteriostatic water, a mixed vial is generally used within 28–30 days when stored continuously refrigerated at 36°F to 46°F (2°C to 8°C). The calculator shows your projected vial days and discard date using this 28–30 day guidance in the optional protocol tracking section.
